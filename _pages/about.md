I am a second-year Master's student at the Auditory Cognition and Computational Acoustics Laboratory ([AudioCC Lab](https://audiocc.sjtu.edu.cn/en)) @ Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. [Yanmin Qian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=guG9lxgAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

Research interests:
- *Speech and audio signal processing*: speech synthesis, speech deepfake detection, speaker verification, audio classification and detection
- *Machine learning*: domain adaptation and generalization, self-supervised, unsupervised and semi-supervised learning
