## About Me

I am a Master's student at the Auditory Cognition and Computational Acoustics Laboratory@Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. [Yanmin Qian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=guG9lxgAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

## Research Interests

- **Speech and audio processing**: speaker verification, acoustic scene classification, sound scene detection, anomaly sound detection.
- **Machine learning**: domain adaptation and generalization, self-supervised learning
